Privacy policy

Last updated: 13 October 2026

What we collect

Mafia God has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Nothing leaves your device unless you use one of the optional services below.

Your game data

Player names, roles and game progress are stored only on your device (in the browser or app storage) so a game can continue after the page is closed. They are never sent to us or to anyone else. Clearing the app data or browser data deletes them.

Permissions

The app asks for no permissions. Vibration is used only for the talk timer.

Children

The app collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Recovery code (optional)

If you create a recovery code, your roles, scenarios, group name and logo are encrypted on your device with a key made from that code and stored on our server (Cloudflare) so you can restore them on another phone. The code itself never leaves your device, so we cannot read the backup. It is deleted 400 days after the last save.

iCloud, Google and Game Center

On iPhone and iPad the same data is also kept in your own iCloud. On Android it is included in the phone’s Google backup if you have that turned on. If you sign in to Game Center, Apple receives the number of games you host and the medals you earn, for the leaderboards and your profile. These services are run by Apple and Google under their own privacy policies.
