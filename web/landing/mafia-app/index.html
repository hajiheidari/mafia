How does it work?

The narrator needs just one phone.

Names and roles

Enter the players. The tool suggests a role mix for that many people, and you can change it.

Secret role deal

Pass the phone around: each player taps their name, sees their role and hides it. The deal is truly random.

Run the game

At night the tool tells you whom to wake, in order, records each choice and has the morning announcement ready.

FAQ

 

Is it free? Do I install anything?

It is free and needs no install. Open it in the phone’s browser, and if you like use “Add to Home Screen”.

Does every player need a phone?

No. Only the narrator’s phone; it is passed around for the role deal.

Is any game data sent anywhere?

No. Everything stays on that one phone and is never sent to a server.

Which language is the tool in?

The tool itself is in Persian for now.

Our house rules are different. What now?

Pick any role mix, and the narrator can remove or bring back a player by hand at any time.
