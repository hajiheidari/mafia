Hello, and thank you for the review. Mafia God is my own original work, not a copy or a template, so I am replying instead of resubmitting.

1. What it does
Mafia God is a tool for the narrator (the "god") of the in-person Mafia party game, as it is played in Iran and in Russian-speaking clubs. A large group plays with many special roles, and the narrator must deal them secretly, wake each role in order every night, remember every choice, work out who survives and run the votes. On paper this is error-prone bookkeeping.

The app does that bookkeeping:
- It deals roles on one phone: each player taps their name, sees their card and hides it.
- It walks the narrator through the night in the scenario's order and records each choice.
- It resolves the interactions (the doctor's save, the Godfather fooling the detective, the sniper's risk).
- In the morning it gives one clear announcement.
- It has a talk timer and a two-round vote counter.

2. Users
People who run Mafia nights: friend groups, families, cafés and clubs. Mainly Persian speakers, for whom Mafia is one of the most popular party games, and also Arabic, Russian and English speakers.

3. What is different
- Real scenarios, not just a card dealer: seven ready-made ones with their own roles and night order, including Iranian club scenarios (such as Takavar and Bazpors), the classics, and Russian sport and city mafia.
- Narrators can build their own roles and scenarios.
- Groups can put their name, logo and colour on the screens and cards.
- Persian first, plus Arabic, Russian and English.
- No account, ads, tracking or purchases. The optional recovery code saves the group's setup end-to-end encrypted: the server stores only ciphertext and cannot read it.

4. Testing
I am an individual developer and tested it myself on my iPhone, in the web version and on Android, running full games in every scenario and language. That testing led to:
- the app now asks for the player count and scenario first
- a two-round vote with a threshold, as real games are played
- iPad support
- group branding, custom roles and saved scenarios
- the language picker first in Settings

5. Standalone or suite
Standalone. This is my personal developer account; Espadna is my brand name. My only published app is Dorehami: Pantomime & Heads Up, a charades and word-guessing game. I built my other games over the past weeks, one at a time, each with its own code, rules and art, and submitted the finished ones close together. That is why Mafia God, Shah Dozd Vazir, Tiaro, Adadi and Ludo Dosti reached review at the same time. I will space out future submissions. Mafia God is the only tool for a narrator, and the only one with night actions, elimination and voting.

6. Could it be part of another app?
No. Dorehami is about acting out and guessing words. Shah Dozd Vazir is a short 4-to-6-player game with no narrator, no night and no voting. Tiaro and Adadi are single-player puzzles, Ludo Dosti is a board game. Mafia God serves the narrator of a long elimination game.

7. Code shared with my other apps
It is its own web codebase (HTML, CSS, JavaScript) in a Capacitor shell, written for this app. One small native helper (about 160 lines) for Game Center and iCloud key-value storage was written for Mafia God first, and Shah Dozd Vazir uses an adapted copy. Build scripts are shared but are not part of any app. The fonts are open source.

8. Third-party code
Only standard frameworks: Capacitor and Apple's GameKit. No template or app-generation service was used. The texts, logic and art are my own.
